# How to read your VALORANT map stats and K/D

[Product guide](../README.md) · [Open your map stats](https://valopeek.app/)

Personal VALORANT map stats separate your results on one map from the overall score. Start with a question: which fights explain a low K/D in an area, or does the pattern change between attack and defence?

## Choose comparable matches

Start with one map and a recent group of available Competitive matches. A single match can highlight a round to investigate. A larger set can reveal recurring locations, but mixing different agents and situations can hide the reason behind a result.

Change one selection at a time. Keep the match set fixed while comparing sides, then try an agent or round phase if it helps answer your question.

## Calculate K/D by zone

**Zone K/D = recorded combat kills ÷ recorded combat deaths in the zone after your selected filters.**

Spike deaths are separate. A zone with kills and no deaths displays infinity; no combat events means no ratio. One or two fights are a small sample.

![Ascent personal map K/D example from three sample Competitive matches](../assets/brand/ascent-zone-kd.jpg)

*Sample export: three matches, both sides, full rounds.*

The sample's overall scoreboard K/D is 49 ÷ 47, approximately **1.04**. B Outside has six kills and one death, a ratio of **6.00**. A Main has one kill and three deaths, a ratio of **0.33**.

Those counts identify fights to inspect. They do not establish your best position or prove A Main is unsafe. Compare the rounds before deciding what to change.

## Inspect a zone

![Ascent B Site selected in ValoPeek with sample personal K/D of 0.75](../assets/screenshots/zone-kd.png)

*Current interface, sample events: three kills and four deaths in B Site.*

Select a zone to inspect the events assigned to it. Where positions exist, the map shows recorded locations. Duel lines can connect your position and an opponent's when both endpoints are available. These are event snapshots, not continuous round movement.

## Add context

Compare attack and defence, then pre-plant and post-plant situations. Custom match selection, agent and role filters can narrow the comparison.

- Does the location appear in several deaths or only one match?
- Does the result change between sides?
- What do available match details and round timelines show?
- Are enough classified events available to support the pattern?

## Understand the limits

Only available current-act Competitive history is included. Matches, positions and context can be missing. Scoreboard totals and filtered event totals can therefore differ.

A concentration of deaths is not death probability: the data does not measure your time spent in every area. Frequent fighting can produce many events there.

Personal agent results are not global win rates. Full-match scoreboard metrics such as ACS and ADR do not follow every event filter.

Instead of “my K/D is low here,” try “let's review these A Main deaths on attack before the plant.” That names a place, events and a situation to investigate.
